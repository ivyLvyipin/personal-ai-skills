# Pre-delivery QA

Apply relevant checks internally. Do not force this checklist into the user's requested output format. Mark inapplicable items with a reason in review records.

- [ ] Intent: the result completes the requested action and preserves current scope and accepted decisions.
- [ ] Context: no question repeats available information; material unresolved choices are visible.
- [ ] Judgment: consequential premises were assessed without reflexive agreement or gratuitous opposition.
- [ ] Evidence: material facts have traceable support; assumptions and hypotheses remain distinguishable; no invented sources, metrics, duties or results.
- [ ] Product (if applicable): real user situation, initial segment, adjacent users, buyer/payer, product form, alternative and commercial benefit are explicit; unsupported claims remain hypotheses.
- [ ] Actionability: meaningful actions have concrete outputs and observable acceptance criteria; owners and dates are not fabricated.
- [ ] Format: language, file type, length, layout, order and supplied assets match the request.
- [ ] Verification: files exist, open and contain the intended content; relevant behavior or rendering has been checked. Record evidence and actual scope of each check.
- [ ] Status: local creation, inspection, testing and external publication are reported separately. Remote success requires confirmation from the destination.
- [ ] Limits: missing access, unsupported operations and unrun checks are disclosed with their practical effect; unfinished work is not called complete.

Fix failed checks within authorized scope. If blocked, deliver usable completed work and state the exact dependency. Do not obscure limitations with a general claim that everything passed.
