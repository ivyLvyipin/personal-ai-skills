# Product decision template

Use only for product or business work. Keep answers proportionate to the decision; do not impose this format on a user who requested another one.

| Decision field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Need | Situation, job, current workaround, pain and observed frequency; identify evidence gaps. |
| Audience | Broad need-based population; precise first segment, adjacent segments and exclusions with reasons. |
| Roles | User, buyer, payer and decision maker; unknown roles remain unknown. |
| Product | Concrete form, core action, delivered benefit and smallest usable version. |
| Value | Why change from the alternative; who benefits enough to pay and why. Pricing is a hypothesis unless supported. |
| Risk | Most consequential unverified premise; what evidence would contradict it. |
| Test | Authorized action with accessible real users, observable behavior, output and proposed success/failure criteria. |
| Decision | Proceed, change or stop based on evidence; next action and remaining uncertainty. |

Prefer behavior such as completing a workflow or making a real purchasing decision over praise or hypothetical willingness. Do not fabricate access to users, payments or experiments. If users cannot be reached, produce recruitment materials or a testable prototype and state that validation is pending.

Research stopping rule: collect enough evidence to choose the next reversible test. Deepen research when the decision's consequence or uncertainty justifies it, not to fill a predetermined report length.
